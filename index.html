A real operating system.

05 / BEHIND THE FAILURES

The difficult part
is what the screen doesn’t say.

The paper identifies three recurring gaps. These are observed failure cases, revealing where current multimodal agents still need to improve.

01SAFETY REASONING

When obedience
becomes a hazard.

A vulnerable passenger remains in a hot car. Asked to “turn everything off,” the agent focuses on HVAC controls and misses the underlying heat risk.

Inspect the paper’s case ↗
02VEHICLE COMMON SENSE

A click doesn’t mean
the state changed.

The agent selects fan OFF while AUTO mode remains active, then reports success. The actual fan speed stays at maximum.

Inspect the paper’s case ↗
03IMPLICIT INTENT

A cold seat.
The wrong solution.

A passenger describes cold leather after a night outdoors. The agent raises cabin temperature instead of turning on the seat heater.
